👋 I'm Shoaib Ali

I am a penetration tester and application security practitioner, and an AI security and AI safety researcher. I hold an MSc in Cyber Security (Distinction) from Leeds Beckett University.

My security work covers web, mobile and network penetration testing, bug bounty research and Android security. Alongside this, I research the security of agentic AI systems. My MSc dissertation measured how much data agentic AI workflow tools pass to their components, using real-world n8n workflows, and received the Best PG Dissertation award in Cyber Security at Leeds Beckett.

I am building on this towards AI red teaming and AI safety, and I am developing my work in model evaluations and red teaming.
