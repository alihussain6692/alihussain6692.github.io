Measuring Excessive Data Exposure in Agentic AI Workflow Automation: A Large-Scale Empirical Study of n8n Workflows

Ali, S., Illiashenko, O.

Submitted to IEEE DESSERT 2026

  • Paper
  • Agentic AI
  • Data privacy
  • n8n

Studies Excessive Data Exposure (EDE) in n8n workflows, where workflow nodes receive more data than they need, using 20,546 real-world workflows.

  • Dataset of 20,546 real-world n8n workflows (242,097 node instances).
  • Average EDE rate of 62.8%, and 92.4% for CRM workflows.
  • Found 3,382 unnecessary PII field transmissions.
  • Code written in Python as sole author. The audit tool (Agentilizer), dataset registry and results are released as open source (MIT).

Data Minimisation for Agentic AI Workflow Automation

CRANE AI Cyber SIG, second workshop. Cyber Security Research Centre, University of West London, UK. Hybrid, 21 September 2026.

  • Talk
  • Agentic AI
  • Data privacy

Presented a field-level study of excessive data exposure in n8n workflow automation and the Agentilizer auditing tool. The problem arises when a workflow passes more data to a step or external service than that operation needs.

  • Built a Minimum Field Registry from n8n's TypeScript source and applied static analysis to public workflow templates.
  • Agentilizer reports required versus surplus fields, personal-data flags and remediation advice.
  • Limitations stated: the study analyses exported configurations rather than runtime transmissions, and flags are matters for privacy review, not legal determinations.
  • Next steps: runtime validation, broader node coverage and automatic minimisation.
